Mission briefing

Route reconnaissance

You supervise two unmanned ground vehicles, UGV #1 and UGV #2. Each drives its own off-road route from the staging area to the objective. Your goal is to get both vehicles to the objective as quickly as possible.

!

Watch for this. If a vehicle misjudges something, it drives on into it and gets stuck (−100 points). After 30 seconds it waits for you to send it round.

1Your screen

Vehicle panels
One per vehicle, side by side: its status light, a short message, and its forward camera, the view through its windscreen.
Spot Report
Bottom right. Count the vehicles and people in each image and submit. A new image appears a few seconds after you submit; one you leave is replaced after 20 seconds.

Status lights

  • Green driving its route
  • Yellow something ahead: it slows down and judges it, still driving
  • Red stopped at something it cannot get past, waiting for you
  • Blue following the waypoints you chose
  • Grey stopped by you, stuck, waiting at a section line, or at the objective

Timing

The route has three sections. At each section line the vehicles wait for each other and the mission pauses for a short check-in. The mission ends at 24:00, even if a vehicle has not reached the objective.

2At an obstacle

The light turns yellow and a short alert appears in the view. The vehicle slows down to judge what it sees, but keeps driving. Then one of three things happens:

  • It can get through: it drives on and the light turns green.
  • It cannot get past: it stops and waits for you (−50).
  • It misjudges it: it drives into it and gets stuck (−100).

What you can do

Stop
Halts the vehicle where it is while you look. No points lost, only time.
Continue
Lets a vehicle you stopped drive on by itself.
1Take over
Click a numbered waypoint in the vehicle's view. It follows them round the obstacle, more slowly, then carries on by itself. Any waypoint works; they all lead round the obstacle. If the vehicle could have got through by itself, taking over costs points.
