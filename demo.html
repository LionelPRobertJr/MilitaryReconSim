Route reconnaissance

You supervise two unmanned ground vehicles, UGV #1 and UGV #2. Each drives its own off-road route from the staging area to the objective. Your goal is to get both vehicles to the objective as quickly as possible.

What you see

Two vehicle panels, side by side. Each shows the vehicle's status light, a short message, and its forward camera: the view through its windscreen as it drives.

Spot Report (bottom right): count the vehicles and people in each image and submit. As soon as you submit, a new image appears; an image you leave is replaced after 20 seconds. There are as many as you have time for, and each correct count earns points.

Status lights

  • Green: driving its route
  • Yellow: something on the track ahead; the vehicle slows down and judges it while it keeps driving
  • Red: stopped at something it cannot get past, waiting for you
  • Blue: following the waypoints you chose
  • Grey: stopped by you, waiting at a section line, stuck, or at the objective

What you can do

When a vehicle spots something on the track ahead, its light turns yellow, a short alert appears in its view, and it slows down to judge it. It keeps driving: it stops by itself only at something it cannot get past, and that costs points. You decide what to do:

Stop
Halts the vehicle where it is, so you can look before deciding. No points lost, only time.
Continue
Lets a vehicle you stopped drive on by itself.
Take over
Click one of the numbered waypoints beside the obstacle in the vehicle's view. It follows them round the obstacle, more slowly, then carries on by itself. If the vehicle could have got through by itself, taking over costs points.

At each section line the vehicles wait for each other, and the mission pauses for a short check-in. The mission ends at 24:00, even if a vehicle has not reached the objective.

Points

  • +200 per vehicle that reaches the objective
  • +1 per second left, if both arrive before 24:00
  • +3 per correct Spot Report count
  • −50 each time a vehicle stops by itself and waits for you (pressing Stop costs nothing)
  • −30 each time you take over at something the vehicle could have got through by itself
  • −100 if a vehicle gets stuck
